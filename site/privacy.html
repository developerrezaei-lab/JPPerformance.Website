This notice covers jpperformance.co.uk. It is not a privacy policy for a future Android or VPN app.

Who operates this website?

JP PERFORMANCE LTD, company number 17542449, registered in England and Wales. Our registered office is Unit 2 Cavendish Retail Park, Keighley, BD21 3RB, United Kingdom. Contact: info@jpperformance.co.uk.

Browsing this website

This website does not include advertising pixels, analytics scripts or a newsletter database. We do not set advertising or analytics cookies. Fonts, styles and illustrations are served with the website.

The hosting and network services that deliver and protect the website may process technical request information, such as IP address, requested URL, browser information and timestamps, for delivery, security and troubleshooting. The domain uses Cloudflare as a network provider. Its processing is described in Cloudflare’s privacy policy.

Fitness tools and saved plans

BMI values and rest-timer settings are calculated in your browser tab and are not sent to us. The workout and meal planners save a plan in browser local storage only when you choose “Save on this device”. Plan imports are read in your browser. These standalone planners do not submit planner content to our server. The member studio described below works differently.

Local storage belongs to your browser profile and may be accessible to other people using that profile. Use “Delete saved plan” in the relevant planner to remove it, or clear this site’s browser storage. Files you export or print are separate copies and are not deleted by clearing the website’s saved plan. Any cloud backup or sync of exported files is controlled by your chosen file provider.

Member studio · private technical preview

The member studio at /portal/ uses a separate account and server database. It stores your email address, password hash, display name, consent timestamp, coach relationships, assigned plans, measurements, meal records and completed workout sessions. Essential HttpOnly session cookies and an anti-forgery cookie support sign-in and protect changes. We do not store web sign-in tokens in browser local storage.

Fitness measurements and food records may reveal health information. Before saving these records, the studio asks for explicit consent. Accepting a coach’s invitation separately authorises that coach to view your recorded progress, meals and plans. Revoke the relationship in Overview to stop future access. Revocation does not remove copies a coach has already lawfully obtained. A coach cannot access unrelated students through the API.

Records are kept while the account and coaching relationship require them, subject to a documented retention and deletion process that must be established before public launch. The initial release does not provide automatic account deletion, data export or email password recovery. During this private technical preview, use fictional test data only. Public registration in production is disabled by default until support, retention, deletion, backup protection and recovery procedures are operational.

Food composition estimates are derived from the UK CoFID 2021 dataset. Searches and meal portions are processed by our API; the food data provider is not contacted for each calculation. Exercise catalogues contain links to external guidance that you can choose to open.

For coaching team administration, an assigned supervisor can see their coaches’ names, contact emails and counts of active coaching relationships. System administrators manage these assignments and role permissions. Supervisory access does not automatically share athletes’ individual health records.

If you contact us

The contact form prepares a draft in your email application. It does not submit the form contents to a website database. If you send the email, we receive the information you include, such as your name, address and message. Please avoid sending sensitive personal information.

We use correspondence to respond to your enquiry and manage related follow-up. Our basis for handling ordinary business enquiries is our legitimate interest in responding and conducting business. Where an enquiry is about a prospective contract, processing may also be necessary to take steps at your request before entering a contract.

Correspondence is kept while needed to address the enquiry, maintain relevant business records or meet applicable obligations. Hosting, network and email providers may process information on our behalf. International processing may occur according to their service arrangements and applicable safeguards. We do not sell website enquiry information.

The JP Fit browser concept

The interactive concept stores demo workout entries and a rest-timer end time in your browser’s local storage so they survive a page refresh. They are not sent to us. This is functional storage, used only for the concept. Use “Clear demo data” on that page to remove it. Avoid entering sensitive health information. Storage can also be cleared in your browser settings.

Your choices and requests

You may ask us about personal information we hold about you, request access, correction or deletion, and exercise other rights that apply to your situation, including objection to processing based on legitimate interests. Email us using the address above. You can also raise concerns with the UK Information Commissioner’s Office.

Future apps and changes

Each released app will need privacy information that reflects its actual features, permissions and services. This website notice does not make claims about future app data practices. We will update this notice if the website changes.
